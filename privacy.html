Privacy

Short version: this site has no accounts and no sign-in. We count visits without cookies. Nothing you type leaves your browser.

Visit counts

Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits. It sets no cookie and stores nothing in your browser. Cloudflare says it does not fingerprint visitors. It sees the page address without anything after a question mark, the site you came from, your country, your browser, your operating system, your device type and how fast the page loaded. To make the request, your browser shows Cloudflare your IP address, as it does to any web server. It does not see anything you type. When you click an e-mail link, the page also sends Cloudflare one fixed word for that click, such as out-mail. It never sends the link address. We see only totals. We cannot see which visitor did what, and nothing follows you from one visit to the next.

Cookies

The site sets no cookie.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub sees ordinary request logs, such as your IP address and browser type, when your browser asks for a page.
