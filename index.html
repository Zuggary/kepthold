Your copy stays in your office

Your question is answered on your own computer. Nothing is sent to an outside AI service, so your payroll and your customers stay with you.

It stops when a file it relies on changes

Kepthold stops a copy run when a file that feeds your copy has changed and nobody signed the change. That covers the files that fetch your data, the settings, the saved files from your apps, the address it reads from and the package versions. In our test, 10 made-up mistakes of that kind were scored. It caught 9.

Proof: an independent test (job LH5) that scored 10 made-up mistakes on sample data. 9 caught. Read how we tested it.

What it does not do

Most of the catches are a changed file that was never signed, so a change you sign without reading gets through. A few places are not guarded yet: the file that records the run state, a profile file of the data tool, extra model files and some environment settings. A few changes stop only at the test step, after the copy.

Own your records, even while you rent the apps.
